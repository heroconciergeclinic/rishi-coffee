Our story

happiness, one sip at a time.

Hi, I'm Rishi. I've always loved making people smile. As a kid that was easy: I was adorable.

Then I grew up, the cute factor quietly packed its bags, and I needed a new plan. Turns out a really good latte does the trick. So here we are.

“I can't stay cute forever. But I can make you a really good latte.”

Rishi, founder & chief smile officer

the plot twist

my day job is putting people to sleep.

my other job is waking them back up.

By day I'm an anesthesiologist. By early morning, late night and most weekends, I'm steaming milk and practicing latte art. Sometimes I even enter competitions to show off my hearts and tulips.

Every bottle is filled, capped and labeled by hand in small batches. Real espresso, real milk and a vanilla syrup I make myself. No mystery ingredients, no months on a shelf. Just a happy little latte waiting in your fridge.
