the fine print

privacy policy.

Effective October 20, 2026

Rishi Coffee ("we," "us") makes small batch bottled lattes in Chicago, Illinois. This policy explains what we collect on coffeebyrishi.com and what we do with it. Short version: we only use your info to send you the Rishi Coffee updates you asked for, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Info you give us. When you sign up for drops, we collect your name, phone number and/or email, whether you agreed to receive texts, and the date and page you signed up from.
  • Your cart. Items you add to the cart are saved in your own browser so they're there when you come back. We don't receive them until you place an order.
  • Basic technical info. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs things like your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site running and secure. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we don't take payment information on this site.

How we use it

  • To send the texts and emails you signed up for: Sunday one-liners and alerts when a new drop is ready.
  • To answer you when you reach out.
  • To keep the site working and protect it from spam and abuse.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Rishi Coffee, such as our website host (Vercel), the tool that stores sign-ups (Google), and our email and text messaging provider. They may use it only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messaging. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up info until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • To see, correct or delete the info we have about you, send us a DM on Instagram at @rishi.coffee.

Kids

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? DM us on Instagram at @rishi.coffee.
